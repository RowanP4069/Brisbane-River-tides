Brisbane River gauges: Breakfast Creek to Moggill

Loading the latest readings…

Measured water levels, as published. Brisbane City Council's raw, unchecked gauge readings, fetched live when this page opens (the council keeps 72 hours). Readings arrive at irregular times, and some gauges' come in batches up to a couple of hours late. Gaps of up to an hour are bridged by a straight line (off by at most a few cm); longer gaps, and gauges that haven't caught up yet, show as no reading. Nothing here is a forecast: for predicted tides use the Predictions page. Not a flood warning service: for warnings see the Bureau of Meteorology.

Heights are metres AHD (Australian Height Datum, roughly mean sea level), one datum for every gauge, so unlike the predictions these do form a real water surface. The predictions page uses each place's own LAT instead, about 1.2 m lower at Port Office, so the numbers differ. Jindalee and Moggill have not read below about 0 m AHD in the readings checked so far: their lowest tides may not be recorded in full.
