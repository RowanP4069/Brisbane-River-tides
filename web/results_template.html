Report the flow (results): what has been seen on the river so far

Every flow report sent from the Report the flow page, together with the project's own field observations, checked against the predictions where there are predictions to check against. It grows as reports come in. Below Kholo Creek the predictions are of water height; a report of the flow is compared with whether the level was predicted to be rising (flow upstream) or falling (flow downstream).

By reach

"Matched" counts only reports that could be checked: not near a turn, not swirling, and below Kholo Creek. It shows as a percentage once a reach has such reports; before that a percentage would look more certain than it is. Devices: reports from the same phone or computer are grouped, so one keen paddler doesn't look like many separate confirmations.

When, relative to high water at the Bar

Each report sits at its place along the river (up the side) and how long before or after the nearest predicted high water at the Brisbane Bar it was made (across). High water reaches places further up the river later, along the line. Over time this shows where and when the flow actually turns.

Hypotheses above Kholo Creek

Above Kholo Creek there are no official tide figures. Anything the project says there is a hypothesis: written down and dated before the data that tests it, with what would prove it wrong stated in advance. The rules: docs/hypotheses.md.

How this works

Each report's verdict is worked out again every time this page is built, with the current predictions, the same way the report page does when a report is sent. So if the predictions improve, old reports show it. When a verdict now differs from the one the reporter was shown, the table says so. Within minutes of predicted high or low water a report counts as "near the turn": the flow is changing then, so any direction is plausible. The confidence lines: below Indooroopilly the predictions use Maritime Safety Queensland's published height ratios; from there to Kholo Creek MSQ gives separate spring and neap figures, which this project averages; above Kholo Creek there are only hypotheses under test.
