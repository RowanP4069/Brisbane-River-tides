Brisbane River tides: Brisbane Bar to Kholo Creek

Predicted astronomical tide only. Excludes river flow, wind, storm surge and dam releases. Colours are proxies for flow direction derived from predicted water level. They are not measured or modelled currents. Not for navigation.

Model limits: upstream of Seventeen Mile Rocks, ratios are estimated, so on big spring tides heights can dip below local LAT. Between Indooroopilly and Seventeen Mile Rocks (see data/README.md) the published lags run backwards, so the tide appears to arrive earlier upstream. Heights are on each place's own LAT datum and are not comparable as a water surface; the slope proxy uses the departure from local MSL instead.
