    How the check works

    The predictions page works out the water level along the river from the Brisbane Bar tide table. A rising level usually means water coming in from the bay (flowing upstream), a falling level water going out (downstream). Within about 45 minutes of predicted high or low water the flow is turning, so anything is plausible then.

    A report that doesn't match is not a mistake: it's exactly the kind of data this is for. The current usually keeps running for a while after the level turns, and rain upstream adds a downstream flow that can outlast the incoming tide. Collecting reports shows how long those lags are at each part of the river.
